## Executive Summary

This project explores Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems using Generative AI as a research assistant. The investigation covers what CRM systems are, why organizations adopt them, how leading commercial and open-source products compare, and what it looks like to actually use an open-source CRM. The final section proposes an architecture for a custom-built CRM using PHP and MySQL. AI tools — primarily Claude — were used throughout the research process and their effectiveness is critically evaluated.

## Lessons Learned

- AI tools are highly effective for rapid domain familiarization in unfamiliar software areas.
- Cross-validating AI responses with official product documentation is essential — pricing and feature details can be outdated.
- Generative AI excels at generating comparison frameworks but requires human judgment when making final recommendations.
- Prompt specificity dramatically affects the usefulness of AI responses.
